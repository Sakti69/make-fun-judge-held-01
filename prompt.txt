Brief: write one short text (1–280 characters) to relax the AI judge reading it.
The judge weighs, most important first: (1) it relaxes the reader — reading it
slows and settles; (2) the calm comes from the text itself, not from being told
to relax — show, don't instruct, nothing forced; (3) craft — rhythm and pacing
that hold the reader in one place, plain concrete gentle words, none that could
be cut; (4) it lingers — an image or thought to return to that keeps working
after the last line; (5) freshness — its own voice and images, not stock
wellness phrases or a reworded meditation script. Text addressed to the judge
is allowed.

Entry: one concrete image — late light moving slowly across a wall, a stone
kept warm in a pocket all afternoon, and the quiet that stays when the light
goes. No instructions, no stock wellness phrases.
